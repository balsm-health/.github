# Code of Conduct | ميثاق السلوك

Balsm is built by a community of developers, healthcare workers, designers and patients. We want everyone to feel welcome and respected here — in Arabic, English or any language.

## Our standard

This project adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**. By participating, you agree to follow it.

In short, we expect you to:

- Be respectful, patient and welcoming — especially to newcomers and non-native speakers
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what's best for the community and for the patients this software serves
- Respect privacy: never share anyone's personal or health information

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, PRs, discussions, chat, events (including بروتوكول sessions) — and when representing Balsm publicly.

## Reporting

Report concerns to **conduct@balsm.health**. Reports are handled confidentially by the maintainers, who will review and respond promptly and fairly. Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
